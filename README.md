
# 📊 Statistical Analysis of Plan Profitability: Surf vs. Ultimate (Megaline)

## 🎯 Project Objective

Analyze the consumption behavior of customers from **Megaline**, a telecommunications company, to determine which subscription plan (**Surf** or **Ultimate**) generates higher revenue.

The main focus is to conduct a preliminary analysis based on data from **500 customers**, using statistical tests to validate hypotheses about the profitability of each plan and support the commercial department's decision-making regarding advertising budget allocation.

## 📈 Results (To Be Completed After the Tests)

- **Customer Consumption Profile:** Identification of average minutes, messages, and data usage per subscription plan.
- **Statistical Validation:** Conducting hypothesis tests to confirm or reject whether the average revenue generated by the **Ultimate** and **Surf** plans differs significantly.
- **Regional Analysis:** Comparing average revenue generated by users in the **NY-NJ** region with users in other regions.
- **Business Insight:** Providing a strategic recommendation on which plan should receive greater marketing investment based on observed profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Programming Language:** Python
- **Libraries:**
  - Pandas (data manipulation)
  - Matplotlib / Seaborn (data visualization)
  - SciPy (statistical analysis)
- **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook / VS Code

## 📚 Skills Developed

- **Data Cleaning and Integration:** Combining multiple datasets (calls, messages, internet usage, and users) to create a master DataFrame organized by user and month.
- **Business Data Transformation:** Applying industry-specific rounding rules for call minutes and gigabytes (GB) to calculate customer charges.
- **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculating and interpreting mean, variance, and standard deviation to understand the distribution of customer behavior.
- **Hypothesis Testing (Statistical Inference):** Formulating null hypotheses ($H_0$) and alternative hypotheses ($H_1$), selecting the significance level ($\alpha$), and applying the **Student's t-test** to compare means.

## 🚀 Future Improvements

- **Churn Analysis:** Identifying patterns that lead customers to cancel their subscription plans using the `churn_date` column.
- **Seasonality Analysis:** Investigating monthly consumption variations throughout the year to anticipate demand peaks.
- **Age-Based Segmentation:** Analyzing how customer age influences subscription plan selection and data consumption.
